ABOUT ME

The story of how everything started...

Hi, my name is Enrique Felipe, and I am the owner and operator of Sioux Diamond Detailing LLC. I started this business in 2023 at just 17 years old, heading into my senior year of high school, with one clear goal in mind: to provide high-quality, honest detailing to the Sioux Center area and surrounding communities.

What started with a vacuum I borrowed from my dad (yes, I still use it today), a few basic supplies, and a vision quickly turned into something much bigger. In the beginning, I detailed out of my old Impala—working anywhere and everywhere, driving hours across Northwest Iowa to serve my customers. Today, Sioux Diamond Detailing has grown into a dedicated shop while still offering mobile detailing services. It has been an incredible journey, and one that I have poured countless hours, effort, and resources into while continually reinvesting back into the business.

Above all, this business has been guided by God’s hand. Through His guidance and the overwhelming support of this community, Sioux Diamond Detailing has grown far beyond what I could have imagined. I truly believe none of this would be possible without Him. While I am proud of how far we’ve come, this is only the beginning. My goal is to continue growing Sioux Diamond Detailing into something greater than I ever imagined—while staying rooted in faith, integrity, and excellence.

I was born and raised in Sioux Center, and I’ve always had a strong work ethic. From a young age, I enjoyed staying busy and working with my hands. What began as a way to make a little extra money quickly turned into a passion for detailing and business ownership. There is nothing more rewarding than seeing a vehicle transformed and watching a customer’s reaction when they see the final result.

At Sioux Diamond Detailing, I hold my work to a very high standard. I don’t rush jobs or cut corners, and if something isn’t up to my expectations, I will redo it—no questions asked. Whether it’s a car, truck, boat, or tractor, every vehicle is treated with the same care and attention to detail. I am always looking to grow, expand services, learn new skills, and invest in continued training so I can consistently deliver the best possible results.

Outside of detailing, I enjoy staying active, spending time with loved ones, and hanging out with my dog. I am a Christian and am incredibly thankful for the role God plays in my life and business. I love giving back to the community, volunteering at church, and attending services with my girlfriend. I also enjoy watching sports and am always working on improving my golf game.

Thank you for trusting Sioux Diamond Detailing LLC. Every vehicle, every customer, and every opportunity is a blessing, and I am grateful to serve this community with faith, hard work, and excellence.
