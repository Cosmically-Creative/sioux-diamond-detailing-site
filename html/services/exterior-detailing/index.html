EXTERIOR DETAILING

Bring your vehicle's finish back to life

Our exterior auto detailing service restores gloss, clarity, and protection to your vehicle’s paint. We start with a careful hand car wash using premium, pH-balanced products to safely remove road grime, salt, and surface contaminants. Wheels, tires, and exterior trim are cleaned individually for a complete exterior detail.

Next, we can add on a clay bar treatment to remove bonded contaminants such as brake dust, tar, industrial fallout, and overspray. This process smooths the paint and enhances shine before applying a high-quality wax or paint sealant for protection against UV damage, water spots, and environmental elements.

The result is a smooth, glossy finish that stays cleaner longer and makes future maintenance washes easier.
