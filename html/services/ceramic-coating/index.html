CERAMIC COATING

Armor your paint and protect it for years

Our ceramic coating services provide long-term paint protection and unmatched gloss. We begin with a full paint decontamination wash, clay bar treatment, and paint correction as needed to remove swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation. Proper preparation ensures maximum durability and bonding of the coating.

We then apply a professional-grade ceramic coating that creates a durable, hydrophobic layer over your vehicle’s paint. This coating enhances gloss, repels water and dirt, resists chemical staining, and protects against UV fading and oxidation.

With proper maintenance, ceramic coatings can last for years, making them ideal for clients looking for long-term protection, easier washing, and a consistently polished appearance.
